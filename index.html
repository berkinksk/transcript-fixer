Transcript Fixer Privacy Policy

Publisher: Berkin Kaynar
Effective date: 6 October 2026

Transcript Fixer is a free plugin that provides instructions to ChatGPT for correcting speech-to-text transcription errors while preserving the original meaning and wording.

How the plugin works

Transcript Fixer works entirely inside ChatGPT. It has no separate server, external service connection, or developer-operated database. Text you provide is processed within ChatGPT to produce a corrected response.

Data collected by the publisher

I do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share transcripts or other personal information through Transcript Fixer. The plugin does not send your conversations to me or to an external service operated by me.

Processing by OpenAI

OpenAI operates ChatGPT and processes information submitted to it. This policy describes my practices as the plugin publisher; it does not replace OpenAI's privacy policy. OpenAI's handling of your information is governed by its policies and your applicable ChatGPT settings. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

This privacy webpage

This page is hosted using GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, forms, or tracking code added by me. GitHub may collect technical information about visits, including IP addresses for security purposes, under its own privacy statement. Visiting this page does not submit a transcript to Transcript Fixer.

Questions and changes

For questions about this policy, open a public issue in the GitHub repository hosting this page. Do not include transcripts or sensitive personal information in an issue. Publisher profile: Berkin Kaynar on GitHub.

If the plugin's data practices change, this policy will be updated and its effective date revised.
